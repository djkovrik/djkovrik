Stain First Aid Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Stain First Aid mobile application (the “Application”), operated by Sergey V. (the “Service Provider”), accesses, processes, and shares information. Stain First Aid helps adults follow conservative, safety-led stain-treatment guidance for washable garments and household textiles.

Privacy summary: Your stain and material selections, treatment records, searches, notes, optional photos, favorites, and timers are processed locally. The Application has no user accounts and does not upload this content to the Service Provider, advertising providers, or telemetry services. Core features work offline. Release versions use basic Yandex AppMetrica usage statistics and Firebase Crashlytics, both enabled by default and independently switchable off in Settings. A minimal privacy-region request and eligible Yandex advertisements may also involve network data, as explained below.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

The Application stores the following information locally to provide its core features:

  • stain source, material, care-label answers, freshness, and previous treatment selections;
  • draft and saved cases, guide and step progress, outcomes, and related timestamps;
  • notes, optional case photos, and favorite guides;
  • timer start and due times, completion state, and notification scheduling information;
  • appearance, notification, analytics, and crash-reporting preferences;
  • local advertising frequency counters and the minimal privacy-region and consent cache.

Library searches are processed on your device and are not transmitted to advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services. The Application provides no account synchronization, app-operated cloud backup, or photo recognition. Device-level backups or transfers, if provided by your operating system, are governed by its settings and policies rather than an Application cloud service.

2. Optional Photos and Permissions

You may choose to attach a photo to a case using the system photo picker or camera. Photos are optional local notes: the Application does not analyze them to identify stains and does not upload them. It copies the selected image into app-private storage and re-encodes it without unnecessary original metadata, including embedded location metadata. The saved case references the private copy rather than the original external photo location.

Camera access is used only when you choose to take a photo. Photo selection uses system-provided controls without requesting broad access to your media library. You can decline or revoke permission and continue using stain guidance, history, notes, favorites, and timers without a photo. Deleting the private copy does not delete an original image you selected from another app or library.

The Application does not require your name, email address, phone number, contacts, date of birth, payment information, or device location permission. It does not request the Android advertising-ID permission or request iOS tracking authorization to access IDFA. Network services still receive information inherent in a connection, such as an IP address.

3. Timers and Notifications

Treatment timers and their progress are stored locally. If you use a timer reminder that may appear while the Application is in the background, the Application requests notification permission when needed and schedules the notification locally. Notification delivery is subject to operating-system restrictions. Denying or revoking permission does not stop the in-app timer or block core features. Notification permissions can be managed in your device settings.

4. Third-Party Services and Network Data

Yandex Mobile Ads SDK

The Application uses Yandex Mobile Ads SDK for non-personalized advertising in eligible contexts. Yandex may process IP addresses, device or SDK-generated identifiers other than advertising IDs, application and operating-system information, network information, and ad impressions or interactions to deliver, measure, and secure advertisements. Non-personalized advertising still involves technical data processing. The Application does not supply stain choices, materials, searches, case records, notes, photos, or local case identifiers to Yandex Ads.

Advertising is limited to at most one inline banner after a guide's mandatory safety information, one banner in Library or History, and an occasional full-screen advertisement after a completed result has been saved and the guide has been exited. Completion advertisements do not appear in the first session and are capped at one per three completed guides and one per 24 hours. Ads are prohibited before the immediate safe action, during an active timer, between a warning and its action, and on private-content, stop, professional-help, or other restricted safety screens. Offline, failed, or ineligible advertisements are hidden without blocking core features.

Before initializing Yandex Ads, the Application checks a fresh privacy-region result. Where that result requires consent, an app-owned form offers accept and decline at the first eligible advertising moment. You may later change or withdraw that choice in Settings → Privacy choices while the current region result requires consent. A decline, unresolved choice, unusable or expired region result, or failed region check keeps advertising disabled. Where the current result does not require consent, eligible ads may appear without a consent form, and this regional consent control is not available. The current consent value is applied before manual SDK initialization.

The current release does not use advertising mediation, Google Mobile Ads, or rewarded ads. Advertising is disabled in the current iOS release configuration until production advertising identifiers for its ad placements are supplied.

Yandex AppMetrica

Release versions use Yandex AppMetrica for basic automatic app-launch and session statistics and related technical SDK parameters. Usage analytics is enabled by default. You can turn it off independently in Settings using the Usage analytics switch. A saved opt-out is applied in the SDK's initial configuration on subsequent launches.

When enabled, AppMetrica may process launch and session timing, app and operating-system versions, device and network information, IP addresses, and SDK-generated installation or device identifiers. The Application disables or does not use advertising-identifier tracking, device-location tracking, automatic revenue and purchase tracking, deeplink/app-open tracking, Profile ID, custom product events, and AppMetrica crash reporting. Ordinary automatic launch/session statistics remain enabled unless you turn Usage analytics off. No searches, stain selections, treatment progress, notes, photos, or other case content are sent to AppMetrica. Debug and other non-release builds use a service that does not send usage analytics.

Turning Usage analytics off stops future SDK transmission. It does not delete information already processed by Yandex or promise to reset SDK identifiers. This switch does not control advertisements or Firebase Crashlytics.

Firebase Crashlytics

Release versions use Firebase Crashlytics to diagnose crashes and improve reliability. Crash reports are enabled by default and can be disabled independently using the Crash reports switch in Settings. Crashlytics may process stack traces, exception types and messages, crash times, app and device information, and SDK-generated installation and session identifiers. Network requests also involve connection information such as an IP address.

The Application does not attach case content, stain or material selections, search queries, notes, photos or their paths, advertising identifiers, or custom user IDs to reports or custom logs. Exception messages can contain runtime diagnostic details. Firebase Analytics and Firebase Performance Monitoring are not used. Crash reports may be queued on the device and sent on a later launch when a connection is available. Disabling Crash reports disables future automatic collection according to the SDK lifecycle; it does not delete reports already transmitted to Google. Debug builds disable automatic crash-report upload.

Service Provider privacy-region endpoint

When there is no valid cached result, the Application requests https://168.222.252.178/v1/privacy/region to determine whether advertising-consent choices are required for the current network region. The service receives the source IP address inherent in the connection and uses network-region information for this determination. It does not request GPS access or receive your stain selections, searches, notes, photos, a stable user/request identifier, an advertising identifier, or your advertising-consent choice.

The response contains only a schema version, a consent-required flag, a policy version, and an expiry. The Application caches this minimal response and, when required, your matching accept/decline choice privately on your device. It does not save the IP address, detected country, or detailed geolocation in that cache. Cached region results are never treated as fresh for more than 72 hours. A changed policy version requires a new choice where consent is required. The request does not block offline stain guidance or other core functions.

5. Purposes of Processing

  • providing offline stain guidance, local records, photos, favorites, and timers;
  • determining whether advertising-consent choices are required and applying your choice;
  • delivering, measuring, and securing eligible advertisements;
  • understanding basic launches and sessions while Usage analytics is enabled;
  • diagnosing crashes while Crash reports is enabled;
  • responding to privacy requests and meeting applicable legal obligations.

6. Legal Bases and Your Choices

Where a legal basis is required, the Service Provider relies on consent for advertising processing where required, legitimate interests in operating a reliable Application and understanding its basic use where permitted by applicable law, and compliance with legal obligations where applicable. A regional advertising choice applies to Yandex Ads; Usage analytics and Crash reports are separate, default-on settings. Advertising acceptance or refusal does not change those two switches. Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing that occurred before withdrawal.

You can use core features without accepting required advertising consent or enabling telemetry. You can manage photo/camera and notification permissions in your device settings. To stop future AppMetrica transmission or automatic Crashlytics collection, turn off the corresponding switch in the Application's Settings.

7. Data Sharing

The Service Provider does not sell or upload your local stain-treatment content. Technical data is processed through Yandex Ads when eligible, AppMetrica and Crashlytics when their respective settings are enabled, and the Service Provider's privacy-region service, for the purposes described above. Information actually held by the Service Provider may also be disclosed where required by law or necessary to address fraud, security threats, or harm.

If you contact the Service Provider by email, your email address and information you choose to include are used to handle your request. Please avoid sending case photos, detailed personal notes, or other sensitive information that is unnecessary for the request. Opening an external source, provider policy, or advertisement takes you to a service governed by its own privacy practices.

8. Retention and Deletion

Local case data remains until you delete it, clear Application storage, or uninstall the Application, subject to operating-system backup and transfer behavior. The Application supports deleting individual cases and photos, all history and photos, favorites, or all app-owned user data. Deleting a case also removes its private photo copy; failed file cleanup is recorded for retry.

Delete all data also resets privacy settings. It removes the local advertising-consent choice and privacy-region cache, resets advertising frequency counters and other preferences, and returns Usage analytics and Crash reports to their enabled defaults. Those defaults are applied to the services immediately. If you want telemetry to remain off after this reset, turn both switches off again in Settings. Deleting only history/photos or favorites does not reset those preferences.

A privacy-region result can authorize advertising only until its effective expiry, capped at 72 hours; an expired or incompatible cached result cannot authorize ads. The corresponding consent choice is tied to its policy version and is removed by Delete all data.

According to Google's published policy, Firebase Crashlytics retains crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting removal from live and backup systems. AppMetrica and Yandex Ads retention is governed by their applicable policies, service settings, and legal obligations. Turning a switch off, declining advertising, deleting local records, or uninstalling the Application does not itself delete information already transmitted to a provider.

Privacy-request correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and comply with applicable legal obligations. For access or deletion requests concerning processing for which the Service Provider is responsible, use the contact below. Requests concerning a provider's independent processing may also need to be directed to that provider.

9. International Transfers

Yandex, Google, and their service providers may process technical data outside your country. Applicable processing locations and transfer arrangements are described in the provider information linked above. Where required, transfers are subject to applicable legal safeguards. Your local case content is not uploaded to these providers by the Application.

10. Security

Core records and private photo copies are stored within the Application's local storage. Network services use encrypted transport. Access to your device and any operating-system backups remains subject to your device's security and backup settings. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

11. Adults and Children's Privacy

Stain First Aid is designed and marketed for adults and is not directed to children. It does not ask for a date of birth or create age profiles. If you believe a child has provided personal information to the Service Provider, contact the Service Provider so the matter can be investigated.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, receive a portable copy of, restrict, or object to processing of personal information; withdraw consent; obtain information about sharing; or complain to a data protection authority. Contact the Service Provider to exercise applicable rights. The Service Provider may need information reasonably necessary to verify and locate your request. Because there are no accounts and your case content is kept locally, the Service Provider does not hold a server copy of that content or an account record linking it to you.

13. Changes to This Policy

This Policy may be updated when the Application, SDKs, legal requirements, or data practices change. A revised version will carry a new effective date, and material changes will be communicated where required by law. Changes to the advertising-consent policy invalidate older choices where a new choice is required.
