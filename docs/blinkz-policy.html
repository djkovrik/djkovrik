Blinkz Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Blinkz Android application (the “Application”), operated by Sergey V. (the “Service Provider”), accesses, processes, stores, and shares information. Blinkz uses the front camera to count blinks, display blink-rate statistics, and provide optional sound and vibration alerts when your blink rate falls below your chosen threshold.

Privacy summary: Camera images and facial analysis are processed on your device. Blinkz does not record photos or videos. Its tracking and telemetry features do not upload camera images, eye-open probabilities, or your blink-history database to the Service Provider or Google. Blink statistics and settings are stored locally, but Android may include them in system backups or device transfers. Google Analytics for Firebase, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Performance Monitoring, and ML Kit may send usage and technical data to Google. These services operate automatically; this version has no in-app analytics or diagnostic collection switch. Blinkz has no user accounts and does not display advertisements.

1. Camera and Facial Analysis

With your camera permission, Blinkz accesses the front camera to show a live preview and analyze frames using Google's on-device ML Kit Face Detection. Analysis detects a face and estimates whether each eye is open. Blinkz uses these temporary results to count blinks and show whether a face is available. ML Kit may also calculate facial contours during this processing. Blinkz does not use facial recognition to identify you, create an identity profile, or authenticate you.

Frames and facial-analysis results are processed in memory rather than saved as photos, videos, or facial templates. They are not attached to analytics events or diagnostic reports. The Application does not access the microphone or record audio.

Camera analysis can run while the camera preview is open, including before you press Start or after you press Stop. Start and Stop control blink counting; Stop does not necessarily close the camera. Tracking can continue in Picture in Picture mode while you use another app. To prevent camera access, revoke Blinkz's camera permission in Android settings; you can also close the Application.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

Blinkz stores the following in its local application storage:

  • blink counts for completed tracking minutes, with the local date and time and a local record identifier;
  • your blink-rate threshold and sound and vibration preferences;
  • auto-start, launch-minimized, and minimized-window opacity preferences.

These records support the statistics charts and your settings. Current session totals, elapsed time, face-availability state, and eye-open probabilities are held in memory for operation. Blinkz has no account-based synchronization, application-operated cloud backup, or history export feature. The Service Provider does not receive your local blink-history database through the Application.

3. Permissions and Optional Features

  • Camera: required for live preview and blink detection. Denying or revoking access prevents blink tracking.
  • Vibration: used for local alerts when enabled. Sound alerts play a device notification sound without microphone access.
  • Display over other apps: requested for optional auto-start, allowing Blinkz to open after a screen unlock or device restart, subject to Android restrictions.
  • Boot and unlock events: checked locally to open Blinkz when auto-start is enabled. Auto-start is off by default.
  • Network access and network state: used by the included Google services for their technical, diagnostic, and analytics functions.

Picture in Picture keeps the tracking window visible while you use other apps. Blinkz does not use this feature or the display-over-other-apps permission to capture your screen, read other apps' content, or collect your activity in other apps. You can change optional features in Blinkz settings and manage camera and display-over-other-apps access in Android settings.

4. Google Services and Network Data

The Application includes the services below. Their network requests necessarily expose network information, such as a source IP address, to the receiving service. Core camera analysis and local statistics do not require uploading your camera content or blink records.

Google Analytics for Firebase

Analytics automatically measures app usage. It may process an app-instance identifier, the Android Advertising ID when available, device and app details, approximate location, installation attribution, and automatically collected events such as first open, sessions, app updates, and screen views. Blinkz does not request precise-location permission. The Application does not add custom analytics events containing blink counts, tracking dates, facial-analysis results, or camera images, and does not set a custom User-ID.

Although Blinkz does not show advertisements, its included Analytics SDK can collect an Advertising ID under its default configuration, subject to device settings and availability. This is separate from the camera permission. See Google's Analytics data collection information.

Firebase Crashlytics

Crashlytics automatically reports crashes. Blinkz also reports selected handled errors, including failures in app operations or auto-start. Reports may contain exception messages, stack traces, timestamps, app and device state, and SDK-generated installation and session identifiers. Analytics events may appear as diagnostic breadcrumbs. Blinkz does not attach camera images, blink-history records, a custom user identifier, or custom content keys. Runtime exception messages may contain technical details. Reports may be queued locally and uploaded on a later launch.

Firebase Performance Monitoring

Performance Monitoring automatically measures reliability and responsiveness, including startup, screen rendering, and supported network requests. It may process installation and session identifiers, app and device details, network type, carrier, country derived from IP information, timing, and request metadata. Network measurements can include a URL without its query parameters, response code, transfer size, and duration; they do not include request or response body content. Blinkz does not add blink-history records or facial-analysis results to performance traces.

ML Kit Technical Metrics

ML Kit performs facial analysis on the device and does not send the input images or resulting facial analysis to Google. Separately, its SDK may contact Google for maintenance and send technical metrics, including app and device information, a per-installation identifier, API configuration, processing latency, and feature or error events. These metrics help Google operate and improve ML Kit. They are distinct from your saved blink statistics. See the ML Kit Terms and Privacy and ML Kit data disclosures.

Further information about Google's processing is available here:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Privacy and Security
  • Firebase Android Data Disclosures
  • Performance Monitoring Network Data

5. Purposes and Sharing

Information is processed to provide camera-based blink counting, local statistics and alerts, retain your preferences, measure app usage, diagnose errors, and improve performance. Google processes the technical and usage information described above through the included SDKs. Android's backup provider may separately process backed-up app data as explained below. Blinkz does not include a separate developer-operated server for uploading your camera content or blink history.

The Service Provider does not sell your camera images or blink history. Information the Service Provider holds may be disclosed where legally required or necessary to investigate security incidents or protect legal rights. If you contact the Service Provider, your email address and the information you choose to send will be used to respond and handle your request. Please avoid sending camera images or sensitive health information unless needed for your request.

6. Your Choices and Consent

You can stop blink counting, disable auto-start and local alerts, or revoke camera and overlay access. These controls do not disable Google Analytics, Crashlytics, Performance Monitoring, or ML Kit's technical metrics. This version does not provide a separate in-app consent prompt or switch for analytics or diagnostics.

Android may let you reset or delete the Advertising ID or limit advertising-related processing. Available controls depend on your Android version and device. These controls do not stop all SDK collection or remove installation identifiers. Uninstalling Blinkz prevents further collection by that installed copy but does not automatically delete information already sent to Google.

Where applicable, privacy law requires a lawful basis for processing and may require consent for particular analytics or identifier uses. This Policy explains data practices; it does not itself request or establish that consent. Contact the Service Provider to exercise applicable privacy rights or ask about the basis for particular processing.

7. Android Backup and Device Transfer

Blinkz permits Android system backup and does not specifically exclude its statistics database or settings. Depending on your device, Android version, backup provider, and account settings, those files may be copied to a cloud backup or transferred to another device. This can include blink counts with dates and times. It is separate from Firebase analytics and diagnostics and is controlled by Android's backup and transfer mechanisms. The Service Provider does not operate or receive those backups. Manage them through your device and backup-provider settings. See Android Auto Backup information.

8. Retention and Deletion

Camera frames and facial-analysis results are temporary processing data rather than a saved history. Local blink records have no automatic expiry in this version. They remain until app storage is cleared, the Application is uninstalled, or the data is otherwise removed. There is no in-app control for deleting individual history records. You can clear all local data using Android's app storage settings. Backup copies may remain or restore data later; manage or delete them separately with your backup provider.

Under Google's published Firebase policy, Crashlytics keeps crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before beginning removal from live and backup systems. Performance Monitoring keeps IP-associated events for 30 days and installation-associated and de-identified performance data for 60 days before beginning removal. Analytics retention depends on the Analytics property's settings and Google's applicable policies; aggregated reports may have different retention. ML Kit metrics follow Google's applicable policies. Clearing local data does not by itself delete data already processed by these services.

To request deletion of information held by the Service Provider, use the contact address below. Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request and meet applicable obligations. Requests concerning provider-controlled data or system backups may also require contacting Google or your backup provider.

9. International Processing and Security

Google and your backup provider may process information outside your country, subject to their terms and applicable transfer safeguards. Local app files use Android's application storage protections. Google documents encrypted transport for Firebase and ML Kit technical data. No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security. Protect access to your device and any account used for system backups.

10. Children's Privacy

Blinkz does not ask for a date of birth or verify age. The automatic SDK processing described in this Policy is not disabled by an age check. Parents and guardians can manage installation and permissions through device controls. If you believe a child's information has been processed inappropriately, contact the Service Provider so the matter and any applicable deletion request can be investigated.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction and the processing involved, you may have rights to information, access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent where consent is the basis, and a complaint to a data protection authority. Contact the Service Provider to submit a request. Because Blinkz has no account and does not upload its local blink-history database to the Service Provider, the Service Provider cannot remotely retrieve or erase that database and may be unable to associate SDK records with you without additional information.

This Policy may be updated when the Application, included services, or data practices change. An updated version will show a revised effective date. Additional notice will be provided where required by applicable law.
